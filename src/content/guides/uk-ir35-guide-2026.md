Understanding your IR35 status under UK Off-Payroll Working rules is one of the most critical tax factors for contractors operating through Personal Service Companies (PSCs). 

For the **2026/27 UK tax year**, HMRC continues to enforce strict compliance criteria for public sector and medium/large private sector client engagements.

This guide breaks down the financial and legal differences between working **Inside IR35** versus **Outside IR35**, income tax and National Insurance impacts, and optimization strategies.

---

## 1. Inside vs Outside IR35: What's the Difference?

| Feature | Outside IR35 | Inside IR35 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tax Status** | Genuine business-to-business engagement | Deemed employee for tax purposes |
| **Payment Structure** | Gross invoice paid to PSC; Tax optimized via salary + dividends | Deemed employment payment; Tax/NICs deducted at source |
| **National Insurance** | Class 2 & 4 (or Class 1 Director + Dividends) | Employee Class 1 NICs (8%) + Employer NICs (13.8%) |
| **Personal Allowance** | Full £12,570 Personal Allowance applicable | Personal Allowance applicable |
| **Expenses** | Business expenses deductible from gross profit | Extremely restricted employment expenses |

---

## 2. Key Criteria HMRC Uses to Determine IR35 Status

To evaluate whether a contract falls Inside or Outside IR35, HMRC and UK courts look at three core pillars:

1. **Personal Service / Right of Substitution**: Does your company have a genuine, unfettered right to send a qualified substitute worker to perform the services?
2. **Control**: Does the client dictate *how*, *when*, and *where* you perform the work, or do you maintain autonomy over project deliverables?
3. **Mutuality of Obligation (MOO)**: Is the client obliged to offer continuous work, and are you obliged to accept it outside the specific scope of the project contract?
